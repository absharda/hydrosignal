Volunteer with HydroSignal

HydroSignal turns river data into reports people can actually use — and it's built by volunteers. Sign up, claim a task, and your hours are logged when your work is received. Every month, volunteers receive a signed letter confirming their hours for school or program service requirements.

Roles

  • River Report contributor
  • Data QA helper
  • Content team
  • School chapter lead
  • Event crew — in-person roles are always adult-supervised

Volunteers under 18 need a parent or guardian's consent. The sign-up form includes a parent/guardian consent section for volunteers under 18.
